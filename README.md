- **100% Canonicalized Metadata:** Unique title tags, meta descriptions, Open Graph, and Twitter Cards across every page.
- **Search Engine Directives:** Comprehensive [`robots.txt`](/robots.txt) allowing legitimate crawlers (`Googlebot`, `Bingbot`, `OAI-SearchBot`, `ChatGPT-User`, `PerplexityBot`).
- **Complete Sitemap:** [`sitemap.xml`](/sitemap.xml) indexing all 9 canonical pages.
- **AI Search Standard:** Machine-readable [`/llms.txt`](/llms.txt) providing direct contextual grounding for ChatGPT Search, Google AI Overviews, Microsoft Copilot, and Perplexity.
- **Schema.org JSON-LD:** Validated `Person`, `WebSite`, `ProfilePage`, `BreadcrumbList`, and `SoftwareApplication` schemas.
