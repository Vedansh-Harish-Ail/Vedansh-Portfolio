Automated News Scraper & Headline Aggregator

An automated web curation engine that extracts, classifies, and summarizes real-time headlines across multiple publications into a distraction-free reader dashboard using Python, BeautifulSoup, and Flask.

Scraper Engine BeautifulSoup & Requests

Cache Layer In-Memory TTL Caching
Hosting Render Cloud

Pipeline Architecture

Modern news websites are heavily cluttered with intrusive ads, paywalls, and heavy tracking scripts that degrade reading speeds.

News Scraper runs automated background parsing routines that sanitize HTML markup, strip tracking parameters, extract editorial summaries, and categorize stories by topic (Technology, World, Business, Science) with zero bloat.
